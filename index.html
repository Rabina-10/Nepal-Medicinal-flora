About Nepal Medicinal Flora

Nepal is rich in biodiversity and contains many medicinal plants with important traditional and medicinal uses. This system aims to collect, organize, and present information about Nepal's medicinal flora. It supports plant information management, geographical distribution, observation submission, and conservation monitoring.
